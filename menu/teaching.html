Teaching Philosophy

Clear Structure, Useful Practice, and Linguistic Awareness

My teaching connects language structure with practical communication. I aim to make phonetics, phonology, listening, speaking, and professional communication concrete through guided analysis, repeated practice, and carefully staged feedback.

When appropriate, I use fieldwork examples, acoustic evidence, and classroom technology to help students notice sound patterns and connect them to broader questions in language and communication.

AI-Enhanced Teaching

Classroom Tools and Listening Practice

I am interested in using AI-assisted workflows for carefully controlled listening and speaking practice, including multi-speaker classroom dialogues, accent-based voices, maritime radio exchanges, and configurable radio effects.

Public descriptions of these tools are available in Software & Resources. Restricted internal teaching materials are not published here.
